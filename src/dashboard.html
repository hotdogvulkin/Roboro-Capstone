Questions this data raises and cannot answer
Each one is a real pattern in the numbers with no explanation attached. If you work in one of these states, you almost certainly know something the dataset doesn't.
Every state in this dataset has the same job and the same deadline. Some miss it constantly; most never do. The split is not where you would expect — it has almost nothing to do with how big a state is, how much it spends, whether its budget is annual or biennial, or how flush its treasury was that year. Budget timing is unpredictable year to year and highly predictable state to state, and that distinction is the whole finding.
